NEXA — Cambodian Developer & Student Community

Welcome to NEXA (Network, Explore, eXchange, Assist), the ultimate collaborative hub designed for software developers, computer science students, and tech enthusiasts in Cambodia. Our platform empowers developers to ask complex coding questions, exchange software development knowledge, and recover lost campus belongings securely.

Technical Q&A and Programming Collaboration

NEXA Q&A provides a fast, structured forum where developers can discuss frontend and backend frameworks including React, Spring Boot, Java, Node.js, Python, PostgreSQL, and cloud deployments. Community members vote on helpful answers, tag discussions by technology, and earn reputation points for contributing high-quality programming solutions.

Whether you are debugging code, designing system architectures, or learning full-stack software development at ISTAD or top universities in Phnom Penh, NEXA connects you directly with experienced mentors and peer developers.

Intelligent Campus Lost & Found Network

Never worry about losing your personal items, student IDs, laptops, or accessories on campus again. NEXA provides a dedicated Lost and Found tracking system where students can report missing items, browse recently found belongings, upload verification photos, and claim their possessions through secure verification channels.

Community Leaderboard and Developer Recognition

Recognizing outstanding contributions across the Cambodian tech community. The NEXA leaderboard ranks top contributors, solution authors, and active campus helpers. Earn achievement badges, build your developer portfolio, and stand out in the tech industry.
